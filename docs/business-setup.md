# Business setup and name checks

The business-name checker has a free local rules layer in `frontend/lib/cac-name.ts`. It gives useful guidance without requiring a paid register lookup. Its output is guidance, not a guarantee that a name will be approved or reserved.

Check the current service definitions and onboarding registry before changing commercial scope. Earlier proposals to expand branding into registration/compliance services were planning material; removing them does not approve a service expansion or establish legal advice.

Any additional lookup must expose unavailable, budget-exhausted and incomplete-data states honestly. Provider capability, coverage and pricing require fresh verification before adoption. Dated comparisons, fee quotations and undocumented endpoints are not current evidence. Do not place a headless browser or unbounded paid lookup on the request path.

Keep paid-provider work behind configuration and a documented budget. A visitor should still receive the free rules result when a provider is missing or fails. Formal approval and reservation belong to the responsible registration authority.
