# Realtors' Practice (realtorspractice.ng)
A property platform: verified listings, agents and a CRM behind one login. Client of the studio.

## Tokens
Royal `#0001fc` (brand ground) · navy `#060930` (ink) · pearl `#eef0f8` (light ground) · white. Outfit for display and body. The mark is an R wrapped round a four-pane window, on a rounded-square tile; the wordmark sets "Realtors' / Practice" on two lines.

## Motion identity
- Personality: confident and clean, with a product-forward warmth.
- Signature gesture: **the listings become the window.** Verified listing cards settle on a royal grid, an iris closes, the four middle listings become the window's panes, and the R forms round them.
- Construction grammars explored: a vortex of white arcs that collapse to a point while four dots orbit and lock into the window (gather, after R29); the window swooping in on a trail, construction lines through it, the R snapping together in quarters, one flip (construct, after R28); listings becoming the window (from the content).
- Page transition: a royal circle opens from the point clicked, four dots orbit and lock into the window with one stretch beat, the dots shrink away and the curtain lifts.

## Approved and rejected
- The client asked for mark-first versions of every concept: the icon and its tile finish, then the name slides out from behind the tile as the mark glides left (2026-10-22).
- Three new ways for the R to form round its window were explored (spiral unroll from the window, growing out with a quarter-turn, the window disc swelling into the tile); "spiral unroll" plays on the homepage.
- Studio work in progress R01 ("Fragmented. Structured. Credible.") used white to royal floods with one word per beat and a property card that cleans itself up as the story goes.

## Assets
Real app screens (dashboard, listings) were recreated for the studio's own films; their figures were placeholders.
