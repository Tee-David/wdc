# DeckActions

The deck card's action row, on `ad-hero`. `hb` is the primary: `ad-act` with a white label, turning green (`ad-act-hover`) with a lift on hover. `hb2` is the outline secondary and the tools (Snooze, Hand over, Open details). Tools stay in one row on desktop; at card widths 471 to 620px they drop their labels; at 470px and below every control goes full width.
