# States

## Loading

- Every page has a skeleton that matches its final geometry: the head, the deck as a hero block, list rows at their real height, rail tiles. Shimmer runs in both themes and stops under reduced motion. Never show invented numbers while loading.
- Figures render real values on the server and count up on arrival.
- Slow actions get a designed moment: payment initialising shows its steps; installing the app shows a progress bar on the install card.
- A button that is working keeps its width and shows a spinner beside its label.

## Empty

Every true first-use empty state has a short explanation, a friendly drawn icon, and one clear next action. Stacked actions are full width.

- Deck cleared: confetti, then the check draws in; "You are clear, Babatope." with the next calendar item, then Plan tomorrow and Bring everything back.
- Filtered no results: "No invoice matches "kora 3"." with Clear search.
- Nothing in a tab: say why it is empty and whether that is good ("Nothing is overdue.").

## Errors and permissions

- Errors say what failed and what to do; keep what the person typed.
- No permission: say who can do it and how to ask, never a blank page.
- Offline: say changes will send when back online; the site's offline page covers full loss.
