# Pages

Primary navigation stays at six pages or fewer per role, with the owner's Blog as the one agreed seventh. Support sits in the sidebar's second group for every role.

## Owner (Admin)

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Today | The deck, Later (5 per page), Money moving, Studio pulse, Cash in, Team today, Renewals |
| Clients | Every client: table with search, filters and saved views; client page with projects, money, files, people, activity |
| Projects | Projects by service: list, board and calendar; project page with tasks, files, approvals, content calendar (social), money and activity |
| Money | Invoices, Quotes, Payments, Payouts, Expenses; reconcile bank transfers |
| Messages | Team threads, client conversations and the support channel in one inbox |
| Blog | Posts, drafts and scheduled; the editor |
| Settings | General, Team and permissions, Money, Notifications, Integrations, Trash, System |
| Support (second group) | Tickets from clients and staff, the knowledge base, questions |

## Staff

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| My Day | The deck with step checklists, All my tasks (table), My week, the content calendar, My projects |
| Projects | Projects in their departments; the same project page, without amounts |
| Clients | Clients assigned to them |
| Messages | Team threads and the client conversations they are part of |
| Settings | Profile, Notifications, Appearance |
| Support (second group) | Knowledge base, their tickets, ask Admin |

## Client

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Waiting on you (deck), What we are doing next, Your files, Billing, Next meeting, Your projects |
| Projects | Each project's progress, approvals, files and its content calendar |
| Billing | Invoices, receipts and paying with Paystack or Flutterwave |
| Messages | Conversations with the team |
| Settings | Profile, People at your business, Notifications, Appearance |
| Support (second group) | Answers, their tickets, ask a question |
